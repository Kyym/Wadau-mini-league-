HOW POINTS WORK
Every frame won = 1 Win. Points are calculated as Wins × 3 (like football). When points are tied, the player with the better frame win% ranks higher.
Pts = Wins × 3
P – Frames Played
W – Frames Won
L – Frames Lost
Win% – W ÷ P × 100
Pts – W × 3
Tiebreaker → Win%
